Nus·Nus

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Nus·Nus (the “Service”) handles information when you use the Nus·Nus mobile app or web app. The Service is operated by tamilarasan, an independent developer (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Information we handle

  • Account and profile information: your name, email address, password authentication data, and any phone number you add to your profile. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in credentials; we do not receive or store your account password in the Nus·Nus profile.
  • Contact matching: if you grant contact access, the app reads contact phone numbers on your device and securely sends batches of up to 100 numbers to check which discoverable accounts match. Contact names and other address-book fields are not sent for this lookup. The submitted numbers are processed for matching and are not saved as a contact list or lookup history. To prevent abuse, we limit searches to 5 calls per minute and 1,000 numbers per day per account. You can turn off “Let contacts find me” in your profile settings. You can also enter a phone number yourself for a one-number lookup.
  • Group and financial information: group names and settings, member names and links to accounts, expense descriptions, amounts, currencies, payers, split details, balances, and settlement records. Nus·Nus records settlement status; it does not move money or verify that an external payment occurred.
  • Chat and images: messages, sender display names, timestamps, and images you choose to share in a group. Group members can view the content available to that group. Chat images are stored in Firebase Storage.
  • AI requests: when you use receipt parsing, the selected receipt image and instructions are sent to the Gemini API through our backend. When you ask the Nus·Nus AI about your account, your question and relevant app context—such as group, expense, member, and balance details—are sent to the Gemini API to generate an answer. AI suggestions can be inaccurate; review them before saving or acting on them.
  • Notifications and device information: if you enable push notifications, we store the device’s Firebase Cloud Messaging token in a private account record. Notification messages may include a group name, sender name, a short chat preview, or expense description and your share amount. Your notification preference is also stored on your device.
  • Service and security data: platform information, account identifiers, timestamps, App Check data where enabled, and App Check integrity signals, and technical records needed to operate the Service, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot failures. Firebase and Google may process network and device information as described in their service documentation.

2. Why we use information

We use information to:

  • create and secure accounts and maintain profiles;
  • provide personal ledgers, shared groups, chat, expenses, and balances;
  • match contact phone numbers with registered accounts when requested;
  • send notifications you enable and open the relevant group or expense;
  • provide AI-assisted receipt parsing and answers when you use those features;
  • protect the Service, enforce account and group actions, and fix errors; and
  • meet applicable legal obligations.

3. When information is shared

  • With group members: information you add to a shared group is available to members with access to that group. Consider this before adding chat messages, images, or expense details.
  • With service providers: we use Google Firebase for authentication, database, file storage, backend functions, hosting, app integrity checks, and push notifications. We use Google’s Gemini API for the AI features described above. These providers process information to provide their services under their applicable terms and privacy documentation.
  • For legal and safety reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the Service, or the public.
  • Business changes: information may be transferred as part of a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of the Service, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information.

4. AI provider handling

AI requests are sent to Google’s Gemini API. Google’s handling of prompts, images, and responses depends on the service tier and terms that apply to our API project. In particular, Google’s terms distinguish between unpaid and paid services. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information to the AI features. Read the Gemini API Additional Terms and Google Privacy Policy for details.

5. Retention and account deletion

We keep account and Service information while it is needed to provide the Service, comply with law, resolve disputes, and protect against abuse. Retention can differ by information type and by the provider delivering a service.

You can request account deletion in the app after confirming your password. Your profile, authentication account, personal ledger, private push-token data, and contact-matching rate-limit record are deleted. Before deletion, you must choose whether each shared group you own will be transferred to an eligible member or deleted with its history.

If a shared group is retained, its expenses and text messages remain with the group in anonymized form. We replace your displayed member and chat sender names with “Former member” and remove your active group membership. Chat images uploaded by the deleted account are removed from storage, and their message references are removed; image-only messages are replaced with “[image removed]”. If a group is deleted, its associated group records and chat images are removed as part of that deletion. Provider backups and legally required records may persist for a limited period under the provider’s retention practices or applicable law.

6. Your choices and requests

  • You can deny contact access and add a member using a phone number instead.
  • You can turn push notifications off in the app or your device settings.
  • You can review or update profile information in the app.
  • You can request account deletion from the profile screen.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or object to the processing of your information. Contact us to make a request.

7. Security and international processing

We use Firebase authentication, access rules, and provider security controls to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Google may process information in countries where it or its service providers operate; the applicable provider terms and safeguards govern those transfers. See Firebase Privacy and Security for information about Firebase services.

8. Children

Nus·Nus is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly permit them to create accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes or as required by law. We will change the effective date above and provide additional notice in the app when required.

10. Contact us
